# Vocab with Mama

A fully automated, AI-powered Telegram bot that sends **one useful English vocabulary word every day** to subscribed users. 🌱📚

## 🤖 Meet Vocab with Mama

Want to improve your English vocabulary without spending hours studying?

**Vocab with Mama** sends you one carefully selected English word every day, along with its meaning and useful context — making vocabulary learning simple and consistent.

### 📲 How to Subscribe

1. Open Telegram.
2. Search for **@VocabWithMamaBot**
3. Open the bot.
4. Tap **Start**.
5. That's it! 🎉

You'll receive your daily vocabulary word automatically.

👉 **Search on Telegram:** `@VocabWithMamaBot`

## ✨ Features

* 🤖 AI-powered vocabulary generation
* 📖 One useful word every day
* 💡 Simple explanations
* 📱 Delivered directly through Telegram
* ⚡ Fully automated daily workflow
* 👥 Supports multiple subscribers
